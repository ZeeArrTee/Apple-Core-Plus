Invented by Cheryl Bradley, Apple Core+ brings simplicity, safety, and joy to every kitchen.

HOW IT STARTED

It all began with a simple frustration in the kitchen.

As a home cook and mom, Cheryl Bradley was tired of messy, time-consuming prep tools especially when it came to coring fruits and vegetables. She needed something better. Something faster, cleaner, and easy enough for her kids to help with.

So she sketched an idea. Then she built a prototype. And just like that, Apple Core+ was born a sleek, intuitive kitchen tool with an automatic release that made prep feel less like a chore and more like a joy.

OUR
MISSION

Our mission is to make everyday kitchen tasks simpler, safer, and more satisfying through beautifully designed tools that inspire confidence, creativity, and joy.

What started as a solution for her own kitchen quickly grew into a mission to help families, schools, and chefs everywhere cook with ease.
